# Asset provenance and production requirements

The JPEG assets in this folder were derived from the generated VEXA presentation image supplied earlier in this conversation (original canvas: 1024 × 1536). They are concept artwork, not verified photographs of manufactured VEXA products.

Two campaign crops and eight product crops are included. Product crops in the source were approximately 78–194 pixels wide. They were resampled to consistent display sizes; this does not add factual product detail. The `-chalk` variants are illustrative recolourings. Product detail views in the interface are crops of the same source asset and are labelled accordingly.

`wordmark.svg` and `favicon.svg` are editable vector/text assets. They use a working brand name that has not undergone trademark clearance. No registration symbol or claim of registered trademark ownership is supplied.

No external stock photographs or font files are bundled. System typography is used in the interface. Production assets should include a high-resolution campaign image, consistent front/back/on-body garment images, real colourway photographs, fabric close-ups, approved alt text and documented usage permissions.
